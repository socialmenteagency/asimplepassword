Made on your device. Sent nowhere.

Each password is put together in your browser with its built-in secure random generator. There’s no server behind it, no account and no log. Close the tab and it’s gone.

Why three words?

A password like found-PLANT-dance-3 is easy to read, remember and type on a phone, and much harder to guess than the ones people usually invent, like John131824 or Iamhappy2023. Those follow patterns every attacker tries first: names, dates, common phrases. Ours are picked at random from around 58 billion possibilities, with nothing about you in them.

Questions

Is it strong enough for my email or bank?

For most accounts, yes: it’s much harder to guess than a typical password. For your email or bank, add a word: set “Words” to 4 or 5. Want to check? Try the strength testers above. Better still, keep it in a password manager and use a different password on every site.

The site I’m signing up for won’t accept a dash.

Type the character it does accept (_ or . or anything else) in “Between words”, or choose none.

Why did my clipboard change?

Your first tap or key press on this page copies the password on screen, so it’s ready to paste. Every new password is copied too.

Do you store my passwords?

No. Nothing is saved except the language you pick, and only in this browser. Every visit starts with the default options. We count visits from the server’s standard logs, with no cookies and no trackers.

Can I check how it works?

Yes. The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check it: github.com/socialmenteagency/asimplepassword
